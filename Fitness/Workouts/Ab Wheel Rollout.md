# Name of Workout

## About

- It involves rolling an ab wheel forward and backward while maintaining a stable and engaged core, helping to build strength and stability in the core muscles.
- Muscles Worked: Abdominals, Obliques, Transverse Abdominis
- Equipment Needed: Ab Wheel

## Steps

- engage abs, posterior pelvic tilt
- Do not arch your lower back. Getting into posterior pelvic tilt helps maintain a neutral spine.
- Goal is to fully target abs only, and nothing else.
- Keep hands straight through the entire movement, avoiding bending at the elbows to ensure the focus remains on the abdominal muscles.
- Retract shoulders and lats to engage the upper back muscles and maintain proper posture throughout the movement.

## Progressive Overload

- **Range of Motion**: Gradually increase the range of motion over time as core strength improves, ensuring that the movement remains controlled and the lower back does not arch.
- **Hold Duration**: Increase the duration of the isometric hold at the extended position gradually over time, which will further challenge the core muscles and improve stability without compromising form.
- **Repetitions**: Gradually increase the number of repetitions over time as core strength improves, ensuring that each repetition is performed with proper form and control, without compromising the engagement of the abdominal muscles.
- **Position**: Gradually increase the difficulty of the exercise by incorporating variations, such as performing the rollout from a kneeling position to a standing position, ensuring that each progression is done with proper form and control, without compromising the engagement of the abdominal muscles.
