# Sexercise

- Sexercise refers to physical exercises that are designed to improve sexual health, performance, and overall well-being. These exercises can help enhance flexibility, strength, endurance, and cardiovascular health, which can contribute to a better sexual experience.
- These exercises can include pelvic floor exercises (Kegels), core strengthening exercises, flexibility exercises, and cardiovascular workouts that collectively aim to improve sexual function, stamina, and overall sexual health.
- Sexercise is known to improve and quicken the flow of oxygenated blood, in higher and consistent amounts, along with other beneficial chemical compounds, to the genitalia, which is important for fertility and important during intercourse.
- Sexercises range from kegel exercise to aerobic exercise and cardiovascular routines. Flexibility for performing contortion specifically for erotic or sexual positions may also be practised.
