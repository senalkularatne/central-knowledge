## Warm Up

- Dynamic stretching
- Light cardio (e.g., jogging, jumping jacks)
- Mobility exercises (e.g., arm circles, leg swings)
- Joint rotations (e.g., wrist circles, ankle circles)

## Main Workout

- Compound exercises (e.g., squats, deadlifts, bench press) first
- Isolation exercises (e.g., bicep curls, tricep extensions) after compound exercises
- If doing cadio always do it after the main workout
- Main workout mindset
  - Be aware of exactly what you need to do for each exercises for optimum results
  - Mind muscle connection
  - Perfect form and technique
  - Focus on controlled movements and avoid rushing through exercises
  - Progressive overlord
  - Prevent injury

## Cool Down

- Static stretching (e.g., hamstring stretch, quadriceps stretch, shoulder stretch)
- Light cardio (e.g., walking) to gradually lower heart rate
- Deep breathing exercises to promote relaxation and recovery
