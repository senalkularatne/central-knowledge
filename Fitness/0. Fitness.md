# Fitness

Goal in general is to:

- Lose fat
- Build muscles
- Build muscles and lose fat fast and efficiently naturally
- Improve overall fitness
- Improve health
- Improve flexibility
- Enhance cardiovascular health
- Enhance endurance
- Mind
  - Improve mental well-being
  - Maintain consistency and discipline in fitness routine
- Lifestyle
  - Balanced and enjoyable fitness lifestyle
  - Achieve a sustainable and long-term healthy lifestyle

## Organization of Notes

- Notes are organized in such way you can read from top to bottom recursively, where prior topic or prerequisite knowledge is presented before subsequent topics, allowing for a logical progression and easier understanding of the material, so literally anyone can follow along and build their knowledge step by step without confusion or having to look up information elsewhere.
- All folders and .md files are numbered from 0 to N. Names follows Title Case convention, where each major word is capitalized, making it easier to read and maintain a consistent naming style throughout the project.

## Writing Style

- The writing style aims to be clear, concise, and informative, providing explanations and context where necessary to ensure the reader can understand the material without prior knowledge.
- Writing style follows consistent formatting. If two related concepts will have same number of bullet points, each bullet point will be on similar topic thus easy to compare and understand the relationship between them, maintaining clarity and coherence in the presentation of information.

## Formatting Conventions

- Headings use Markdown syntax with `#` for different levels, ensuring a clear hierarchy and easy navigation through the document. Do not use bold formatting for headings, as the Markdown syntax already provides emphasis and structure.
