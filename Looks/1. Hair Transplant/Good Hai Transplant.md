## Correct Angle and Direction

No, an upward-sitting hair transplant will not automatically become flat over time. The final direction and angle of the hair are permanently set by the angle at which the surgeon makes the recipient incision in the scalp.

So, the key to achieving the desired hair direction and angle is to ensure that the surgeon makes precise recipient incisions according to the natural growth pattern of your hair.

## Natural Density
