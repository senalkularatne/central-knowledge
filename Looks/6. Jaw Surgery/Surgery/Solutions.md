## Best Approach to Jaw Surgery

Must not be superficial fixes instead focus on addressing the underlying skeletal and structural issues so you look like how you were naturally intended to look, maintaining harmony and balance in your facial features. And also not long term maintenance post surgery and full recovery. The goal is to achieve a natural and harmonious facial appearance that aligns with your inherent skeletal structure, rather than temporary or cosmetic enhancements that do not address the root cause of the issue.

## Types of Jaw Surgery

Even though you have many terms, they all boil down to either moving the chin only (Genioplasty) or moving the entire jaw/bite mechanism (Orthognathic Surgery).

---

### Full Jaw & Bite Modifications (Orthognathic Surgery)

These procedures move the larger skeletal structures of the face, affecting the airway, entire facial profile, and how the upper and lower teeth align (the bite).

#### Upper Jaw Surgery: LeFort I Osteotomy (Advancement or Setback)
A surgical procedure that cuts and repositionally shifts the upper jaw bone (maxilla) forward (advancement) to correct midface deficiencies and underbites, or backward (setback) to correct severe overprotrusion and vertical anomalies like gummy smiles.

#### Upper Jaw Surgery: Clockwise (CW) Maxillary Rotation
A technique applied strictly to the upper jaw (maxilla) during a LeFort I procedure. The upper jaw is tilted downward and backward independently to correct a sloped bite plane (occlusal cant) or adjust upper tooth display.

#### Upper Jaw Surgery: Counter-Clockwise (CCW) Maxillary Rotation
A technique applied strictly to the upper jaw (maxilla) during a LeFort I procedure. The upper jaw is tilted upward and forward independently to level an asymmetrical bite plane (occlusal cant) and optimize the aesthetic smile arc.

#### Lower Jaw Surgery: Bilateral Sagittal Split Osteotomy (BSSO) Mandibular Advancement ⭐️
A surgical procedure where the lower jaw (mandible) is split bilaterally and advanced forward. It is primarily used to correct severe overbites (receded lower jaw) and to open up the lower airway space.

#### Lower Jaw Surgery: Bilateral Sagittal Split Osteotomy (BSSO) Mandibular Setback
A surgical procedure where the lower jaw (mandible) is split bilaterally and moved backward. It is primarily used to correct severe underbites (protruding lower jaw) and reduce lower facial projection.

#### Single-Jaw Surgery: Clockwise (CW) Mandibular Rotation
A technique applied strictly to the lower jaw (mandible) during a single-jaw procedure (BSSO). It rotates the lower jaw downward and backward to adjust the bite plane independently without changing the upper jaw position.

#### Single-Jaw Surgery: Counter-Clockwise (CCW) Mandibular Rotation ⭐️
A technique applied strictly to the lower jaw (mandible) during a single-jaw procedure (BSSO). It rotates the lower jaw upward and forward to increase chin projection and manage lower bite clearance independently without changing the upper jaw position.

#### Double-Jaw Surgery: Maxillomandibular Advancement (MMA)
A comprehensive orthognathic procedure involving surgical cuts to both the upper jaw (maxilla) and lower jaw (mandible). Moving both structures forward simultaneously expands the entire airway, corrects bite alignment issues, and re-proportions the mid-and-lower face.

#### Double-Jaw Surgery: Maxillomandibular Setback (MMS)
A comprehensive orthognathic procedure involving surgical cuts to both jaws where the entire complex is shifted backward simultaneously. It is used to correct severe class III skeletal discrepancies (underbites) while maintaining correct dental occlusion.

#### Double-Jaw Surgery: Clockwise (CW) Maxillomandibular Rotation
An advanced manipulation technique used during double-jaw surgery. By rotating the jaw complex in a clockwise direction, the surgeon tilts the jaw structures downward and backward to correct a high occlusal plane angle, reduce an overly prominent chin profile, and lengthen a vertically short face.

#### Double-Jaw Surgery: Counter-Clockwise (CCW) Maxillomandibular Rotation
An advanced manipulation technique used during double-jaw surgery. By rotating the jaw complex in a counter-clockwise direction, the surgeon can increase forward chin projection, flatten a steep jawline angle, shorten a vertically long face, and maximize airway expansion behind the tongue.

#### Segmental Jaw Surgery: Multi-Piece Osteotomy (Maxillary or Mandibular)
An advanced variant of orthognathic surgery where the upper or lower jaw is cut and divided into two or three separate bone segments during the operation. This allows the surgeon to independently widen, narrow, or level the dental arches to achieve perfect alignment when the jaw shape itself is deformed.

#### Transverse Skeletal Modification: Surgically Assisted Rapid Palatal Expansion (SARPE)
A combination of localized surgical cuts along the upper jaw's midline suture followed by the mechanical activation of an internal orthodontic distractor. It physically splits and expands a narrow skeletal palate over several weeks to fix crossbites and severe crowding without altering front-to-back bite alignment.

### Chin-Only Modifications (Genioplasty)

Genioplasty is a specific type of cosmetic or reconstructive surgery that only alters the chin button (the very front part of the lower jaw). It does not change how your teeth fit together (your bite).

The confusion comes because surgeons often combine a genioplasty with larger jaw surgeries to balance out a patient’s facial proportions or improve their airway.

#### Reduction Genioplasty

A variation of chin surgery where a wedge of bone is shaved down or removed backward/upward to reduce an overly prominent, long, or wide chin.


#### Vertical Genioplasty

The chin bone is cut and either spaced apart to lengthen the face, or a wedge of bone is removed to shorten the face.

#### Balcony Genioplasty

A specific variation of a sliding genioplasty where the bone is shifted forward in a way that the top edge of the cut segment rests on the bottom edge of the main jawbone like a balcony, providing a stable shelf for strong forward projection.

#### Horizontal Sliding Genioplasty ⭐️

The chin bone is cut and slid forward (to fix a weak chin) or backward (to reduce a prominent chin).

#### Chin Shield Osteotomy

A modified cut designed to protect the nerves and reshape the front contours of the chin, often used to widen, narrow, or smooth out the chin bone while avoiding an unnatural step-off look.

#### Opening Wedge Genioplasty with Interpositional Grafting

A surgical technique where the chin bone is cut and a wedge-shaped gap is created to advance the chin. The gap is filled with a bone graft (interpositional graft) to maintain stability and promote bone healing, allowing for controlled forward movement and enhancement of the chin projection.
