## Relapse

In some cases, relapse can occur, where the jaw may gradually move back towards its original position after surgery. This can be influenced by factors such as the type of surgery performed, the stability of the bone segments, and the patient's adherence to post-operative care instructions.

## Anesthesia Complications

As with any surgical procedure requiring anesthesia, there are risks associated with the use of anesthesia during jaw surgery. Complications can include adverse reactions to the anesthesia, respiratory issues, and other anesthesia-related risks. These complications are rare but can be serious, and appropriate precautions are taken to minimize these risks.
