# Medical Terms

#### Outpatient

An outpatient procedure is a medical procedure or surgery that does not require an overnight stay in a hospital. Patients typically arrive for the procedure, undergo the necessary treatment, and are discharged on the same day.

#### Local Anesthesia

Local anesthesia is a type of anesthesia in which a specific area of the body is numbed to prevent pain during a medical procedure or surgery. Unlike general anesthesia, local anesthesia does not induce unconsciousness, and the patient remains awake and alert during the procedure.
