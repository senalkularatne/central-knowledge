# Investment Funds

An investment fund pools money from multiple investors and invests it on their behalf. The fund is the arrangement that holds and manages investments; the investor's ownership instrument is introduced in [Investment Fund Shares](../6.%20Financial%20Instruments/1.%20Non-Derivative%20Instruments/5.%20Equity%20and%20Investment%20Fund%20Shares/Investment%20Fund%20Shares.md).

This section explains how funds are organized and managed. A fund is not a new kind of asset; it can hold company shares, debt securities, or other investments.
