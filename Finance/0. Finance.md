# Finance

## What is Finance?

Finance refers to the management, movement, investment, and raising of money.

Investing means committing money now in the hope of receiving more money or other benefits later. The outcome is not guaranteed.

## Primary Branches

Based on the entity managing the money, finance is divided into three primary branches:

- [Public Finance](7.%20Public%20Finance/0.%20Public%20Finance.md): governments.
- [Corporate Finance](8.%20Corporate%20Finance/0.%20Corporate%20Finance.md): companies.
- [Personal Finance](9.%20Personal%20Finance/0.%20Personal%20Finance.md): individuals and households.
