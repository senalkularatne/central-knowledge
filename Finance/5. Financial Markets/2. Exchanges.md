# Exchanges

An exchange is an organized trading venue, introduced in [Market Mechanics](1.%20Market%20Mechanics.md#exchanges-and-otc-trading).

## Examples of Exchanges

- New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).
- Nasdaq.
- London Stock Exchange (LSE).

These are examples, not a ranking or a complete list.

## Exchange Operators

An exchange group operates exchanges. Japan Exchange Group (JPX), for example, is a group rather than the name of a single trading venue.
