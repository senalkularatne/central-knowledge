# Financial Markets

A financial market brings together people or organizations buying and selling financial instruments. It includes the trading arrangements, participants, and rules, not just a physical location.

Instrument definitions belong in [Financial Instruments](../4.%20Financial%20Instruments/0.%20Financial%20Instruments.md). This section explains how transactions in them happen.
