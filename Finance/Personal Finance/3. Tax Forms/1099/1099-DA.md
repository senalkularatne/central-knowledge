Did you receive, sell, exchange or dispose of a digital asset?
I received a 1099-DA or I received (not bought), sold, exchanged, or disposed of a digital asset.

What is a digital asset?

The IRS requires taxpayers to report all transactions involving any type of digital asset, not just virtual currency.

Digital assets include:

    Non-fungible tokens (NFTs)
    Virtual currency, e.g. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin, etc.
    Stablecoins
