# Currency

Currency can mean a monetary unit, such as the U.S. dollar. In this folder, currency means physical banknotes and coins issued for use as money.

Holding a banknote is different from holding a bank-account balance expressed in the same currency. The first is physical currency; the second is a deposit.

## Currency as an Asset

Currency held is a financial asset. Its monetary value does not come from the paper or metal's value as a raw material. A banknote is not a commodity, even though it is a physical object.

Its purchasing power can change through [inflation](../../2.%20Money%20and%20Banking/0.%20Money%20and%20Banking.md#inflation).
