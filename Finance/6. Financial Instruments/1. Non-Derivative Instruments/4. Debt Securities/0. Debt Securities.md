# Debt Securities

Debt securities represent borrowing by an issuer, such as a company or government. The issuer is the borrower; the investor is a creditor rather than an owner.

Unlike an ordinary [loan agreement](../3.%20Loans.md), a debt security is designed to be sold from one investor to another. Whether it is also a "security" under U.S. law is a separate legal question.

Government and corporate issuers are a different way of classifying these instruments. A government bond and a corporate bond are both bonds.
