# Equity and Investment Fund Shares

This folder introduces company ownership and interests in pooled investments. Owning a share of a company or fund is different from owning the things the company or fund owns.

## Equity

Equity means an ownership interest. In accounting, a company's equity is the interest remaining in its assets after deducting its liabilities. It differs from a creditor's contractual claim to repayment.

## Classification Boundaries

Stocks and fund shares are grouped together here because the IMF classification places them in one family. This does not mean that every fund invests in company shares, or that every fund share counts as equity in the fund's own accounts.

Something called a "share" is not always equity. If its terms require the issuer to repay it, accounting can treat it as a liability. Its terms decide, not its name.
