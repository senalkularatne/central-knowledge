# Investment Fund Shares

An investment fund share is an ownership interest in a pool of investments held on behalf of multiple investors. Depending on the fund's legal form, the interest may be called a share, unit, or partnership interest.

The investor owns the fund interest; the fund owns the underlying investments. Buying a fund share is different from buying each of those investments directly.

A fund may hold stocks, debt securities, or other assets. A fund's ownership interests and the investments it holds are different things: not every fund invests in equities.

## Value of the Interest

The value of the fund's assets minus its liabilities is its net asset value. Net asset value per share divides that amount by the number of fund shares.

The terms of the fund determine how investors acquire, sell, or redeem their interests. Redeeming means returning an interest to the fund in exchange for the amount or assets due under its terms.
