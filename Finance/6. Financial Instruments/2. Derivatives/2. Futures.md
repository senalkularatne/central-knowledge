# Futures

A futures contract is a standardized agreement to buy or sell an underlying item at an agreed price for a specified future delivery or settlement. Unlike the buyer of an [option](1.%20Options.md), both sides take on obligations.

Some contracts settle through delivery of the item; others settle in cash. Traders can often close their positions before settlement with an offsetting trade.

Each side must put down a deposit called initial margin, which is only a fraction of the contract's full value. Gains and losses are based on the full contract value, so losses can exceed the deposit.
