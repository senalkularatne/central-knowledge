# Risk and Return

## Return

A return is the gain or loss from an investment, including income it pays. A rise in price and a payment received from an investment are different ways of contributing to its return.

## Investment Risk

Investment risk is uncertainty that could harm your financial outcome. That includes losing money, not receiving expected income, or being unable to sell when the money is needed.

Accepting more risk does not guarantee a higher return.

### Examples

- Business risk: a company's operations or finances deteriorate.
- Market risk: prices fall across a wider market.
- Liquidity risk: selling promptly at an acceptable price is difficult.
- Inflation risk: the money received buys less as prices rise.

These are examples, not a complete list.

## Volatility

Volatility describes how much prices fluctuate. It is one aspect of risk, not the whole of it: a permanent loss and a temporary price swing are not the same thing.

## Diversification

Diversification means spreading investments across different holdings and sources of risk. It can reduce dependence on one investment but cannot prevent every loss. Several holdings that depend on the same industry can still suffer together.
