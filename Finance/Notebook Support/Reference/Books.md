# Books

- Security Analysis by Benjamin Graham & David Dodd
Trains you to spot hidden asset value, earnings power, and balance sheet resilience before the broader market prices in a company's potential.

- Psychology of Money by Morgan Housel

- Competition Demystified by Bruce Greenwald & Judd Kahn
Shows you how to prove whether a company has a real economic moat (economies of scale, customer captivity) capable of protecting high returns over decades.

- Competitive Strategy by Michael E. Porter
Helps you evaluate whether an emerging company is positioned to dominate a fragmented or growing industry rather than getting crushed by competitors.

- Quality of Earnings by Thornton L. O'Glove
Prevents you from getting wiped out by fake growth—teaching you to check accounts receivable, inventory, and cash flow divergences before betting on high-growth stocks.

Quantitative Value by Wesley R. Gray & Tobias E. Carlisle (2012)
• Feature Engineering: Translates qualitative accounting checks (accruals, Beneish M-Score, Piotroski F-Score) into programmable binary or ranked variables.
• Base Rates: Demonstrates how to construct and benchmark factor portfolios against historical comparison groups.

The Theory of Investment Value by John Burr Williams (1938)
• Why it is timeless: Proved mathematically that a stock's value is strictly the present value of its future cash flows.
• Role in your app: Defines the exact terminal math required to separate a stock that keeps a 10x gain from a speculative bubble that crashes back down.

Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation by Stephen H. Penman (2001–2013)
• Filing Data Extraction: An academic accounting textbook showing how to reformulate GAAP statements to separate operating profitability from financing noise.
• Catalyst & Inflection Signals: Helps model operating leverage and return on net operating assets (RNOA) before earnings inflect.

Advances in Financial Machine Learning by Marcos López de Prado (2018)
• Comparison Group Integrity: Essential for preventing false discoveries when testing hypotheses across historical cohorts.
• Signal Validation: Covers purged k-fold cross-validation, triple-barrier labeling (useful for your "10x within 1–5 years and keeps gains" target), and feature importance.

Expected Returns by Antti Ilmanen (2011)
• Cross-Sectional Signals: An encyclopedic, institutional-grade reference on historical return drivers (value, momentum, quality, illiquidity, and ownership/flow effects).
• Regime Conditioning: Helps test whether your 10x signals work across macro environments or only in specific liquidity regimes.

Quantitative Equity Portfolio Management by Ludwig B. Chincarini & Daehwan Kim (2006)
• Signal Construction: Details the math behind factor neutralization, z-scoring across peer groups, and combining non-price signals (like institutional ownership changes and earnings revisions).

• Diffusion of Innovations by Everett M. Rogers (1962)
	• Why it is timeless: Established the empirical S-curve laws of how new technologies and products spread through populations and industries.
	• Role in your app: Models the exact inflection point where early catalysts transition into explosive 1–5 year revenue compounding.

• The Design of Experiments by R.A. Fisher (1935)
	• Why it is timeless: Invented the modern rules of randomized control groups, null hypotheses, and statistical significance.
	• Role in your app: Directly governs your core rule of testing every signal against comparison groups before moving capital, ensuring you do not mistake coincidence for causation.

• Creating Shareholder Value by Alfred Rappaport (1986)
	• Why it is timeless: Isolated the seven fundamental financial drivers (sales growth, operating margin, incremental capital, cost of capital, and competitive advantage duration) that create real equity value.
	• Role in your app: Gives your engine the exact fundamental variables needed to sustain a 10x move over a multi-year horizon.
