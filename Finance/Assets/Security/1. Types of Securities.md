## Types of Securities

There are four primary types of securities: Equity, Debt, Derivatives, and Hybrid.

### 1. Equity Securities (Ownership)

Financial assets that represent fractional ownership in a business entity.

#### Examples

- Common Stock: Standard shares providing voting rights and capital growth potential.
- Preferred Stock: Shares providing no voting rights but offering fixed, prioritized dividend payments.
- Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs): Investment funds traded on stock exchanges that hold a basket of underlying equities.
- Mutual Funds (Equity-based): Professionally managed pooled investment vehicles that issue redeemable shares representing a portfolio of stocks.
- Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs): Corporations that own, operate, or finance income-producing real estate, trading like regular stocks.
- American Depositary Receipts (ADRs): Certificates issued by a U.S. bank representing shares in a foreign stock, allowing domestic trading of international companies.
