# Bond

## What is a Bond?

- A bond is a debt security that represents a creditor relationship. 
- When you buy a bond, you are lending money to an issuer (a government or corporation) in exchange for regular interest payments and the return of your original loan amount.
- Note things like house loan agreements or car loans are also forms of debt, but they are not typically referred to as bonds. Bonds are formalized debt instruments that are traded in financial markets.

## Why the Term "Bond"?

- The word "bond" is used because it literally means to bind or tie someone to a legal agreement. It comes from the old English word for a physical rope, chain, or fastening device that locks things together. Over centuries, the word evolved from describing a physical tie into a legal tie.
