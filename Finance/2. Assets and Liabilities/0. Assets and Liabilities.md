# Assets and Liabilities

An asset is something of economic value that a person or organization owns or controls. A liability is an obligation owed to someone else. These describe the position of a particular person or organization.

For example, money lent to someone creates a repayment claim for the lender and an obligation for the borrower. The lender has an asset; the borrower has a liability. The loan agreement itself is explained later under Financial Instruments.
