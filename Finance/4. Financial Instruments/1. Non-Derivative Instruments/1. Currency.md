# Currency

Currency can mean a monetary unit, such as the U.S. dollar. In this instrument classification, currency means physical banknotes and coins issued for use as money.

Holding a banknote is different from holding a bank-account balance expressed in the same currency. The first is physical currency; the second is a deposit.

## Currency as an Asset

Currency held is a financial asset. Its monetary value does not come from the paper or metal's value as a raw material. It therefore does not belong under physical commodities merely because a note or coin is tangible.

Its purchasing power can change through [inflation](../../1.%20Money%20and%20Banking/0.%20Money%20and%20Banking.md#inflation).
