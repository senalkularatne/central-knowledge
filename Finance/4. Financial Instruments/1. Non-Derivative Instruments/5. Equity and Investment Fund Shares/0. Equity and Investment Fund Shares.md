# Equity and Investment Fund Shares

This branch introduces company ownership and interests in pooled investments. It separates the interest an investor owns from the assets held by the company or fund.

## Equity

Equity means an ownership interest. In accounting, a company's equity is the interest remaining in its assets after deducting its liabilities. It differs from a creditor's contractual claim to repayment.

## Classification Boundaries

The combined heading follows the broad instrument-family distinction used in financial statistics. It does not mean that every fund invests in company equity, or that every fund interest is classified as equity in its issuer's accounts.

Some instruments called shares have repayment or other terms that affect their accounting classification. This introduction is not a rule that the word "share" always settles whether an instrument is equity or a liability.
