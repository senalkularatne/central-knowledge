# Debt Securities

Debt securities represent borrowing by an issuer, such as a company or government. The issuer is the borrower; the investor is a creditor rather than an owner.

In the instrument-form classification used here, these claims are designed to be transferable between investors. They differ from the ordinary [loan agreements](../3.%20Loans.md) introduced earlier. This statistical distinction does not replace the broader legal definitions of a security.
