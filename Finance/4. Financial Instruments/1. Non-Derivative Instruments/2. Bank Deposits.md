# Bank Deposits

A bank deposit is an amount the bank owes its account holder. The account holder has a claim on the bank, rather than ownership of a particular set of banknotes stored in a separate box.

It is a financial asset for the account holder and a liability for the bank.

## Withdrawal Terms

- A demand deposit is available on demand, subject to the account's terms.
- A term deposit is placed for an agreed period. Early withdrawal, when allowed, may have conditions or penalties.

Whether a deposit pays [interest](../../1.%20Money%20and%20Banking/0.%20Money%20and%20Banking.md#interest-and-compounding), and how much, depends on its terms.

## Scope

This note introduces ordinary bank deposits. Tradable certificates and other securities issued by banks need to be distinguished from these deposit agreements.

Deposit insurance is a separate question from the instrument's form. See [Bank Regulation](../../1.%20Money%20and%20Banking/Bank%20Regulation.md) for the introductory U.S. oversight map.
