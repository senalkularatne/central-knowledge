# Loans

A loan agreement supplies money from a lender to a borrower who agrees to repay it. The lender is a creditor: someone to whom money is owed.

This note covers ordinary, non-negotiable loan agreements, meaning agreements not designed to trade as securities between investors. Borrowing through debt securities is a different instrument form in this reference.

## Loan Terms

- Principal: the original amount lent, or the amount of it still outstanding.
- [Interest](../../1.%20Money%20and%20Banking/0.%20Money%20and%20Banking.md#interest-and-compounding): the charge for using the borrowed money.
- Repayment schedule: when payments are due and what they cover.
- Maturity: when the remaining amount is due under the agreement.

The contract may also require fees. Default means failing to meet a required obligation, such as making a payment when due.

## Two Sides of the Agreement

The lender holds a repayment claim, which is an asset. The borrower has a liability. A promise to repay is not a guarantee that repayment will occur.
