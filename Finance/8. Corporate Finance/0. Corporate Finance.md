# Corporate Finance

## What is Corporate Finance?

- Corporate Finance is how companies raise, invest, and manage money.
- It comes before Personal Finance because investing in a company requires reading its financial statements and valuing it.

## Initial Public Offering (IPO)

An initial public offering is the first time a company offers its shares to the general public. It can raise money for the company through newly issued shares and can also let existing owners sell some of their shares.

Buying shares from another investor after public trading begins is different from buying in the offering: that sale generally pays the selling investor, not the company. An IPO is not a guarantee that the investment will rise in value.
