# Financial Assets

A financial asset is money, a right to receive money or another financial asset, or an ownership interest in another organization. Its value does not come from using a physical object such as a building.

Examples include currency held, a bank deposit, a lender's claim to repayment, and shares representing company ownership. Not every financial asset can readily be sold to another person.

A banknote is a financial asset even though the paper is physical. A claim on someone else can also be an asset even when it is recorded electronically rather than printed.

## Relationship to Financial Instruments

A financial instrument is the agreement itself, such as a loan contract, and its terms. "Asset" describes the instrument from the holder's side. The same instrument can create an asset for one party and a liability for another.
