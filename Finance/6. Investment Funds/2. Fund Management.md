# Fund Management

Fund management concerns how investments are selected and maintained. It is a different question from the trading and redemption arrangements described in [Fund Structures](1.%20Fund%20Structures.md).

## Index Funds

A market index measures the performance of a selected group of investments. An index fund seeks to track the returns of a particular index, rather than choose investments independently of it.

An index fund can use an ETF or a conventional mutual-fund arrangement. "Index fund" describes its management approach, not a separate sibling structure beside ETFs and mutual funds.

## Active Management

An actively managed fund relies on a manager's investment choices to pursue the fund's stated objective, rather than simply tracking an index. Active management does not guarantee better returns.

## Costs and Risk

Both approaches involve investment risk and costs. Not every index fund is cheaper than every actively managed fund. The actual investments, fees, and fund terms matter.
