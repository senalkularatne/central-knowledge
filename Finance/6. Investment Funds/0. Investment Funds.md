# Investment Funds

An investment fund pools money from multiple investors and invests it on their behalf. The fund is the arrangement that holds and manages investments; the investor's ownership instrument is introduced in [Investment Fund Shares](../4.%20Financial%20Instruments/1.%20Non-Derivative%20Instruments/5.%20Equity%20and%20Investment%20Fund%20Shares/Investment%20Fund%20Shares.md).

This section concerns the fund's organization and management, not a new type of underlying asset. A fund can hold company shares, debt securities, or other investments.
