### What can increase property tax

#### Heavy Reliance on Local Schools

- Public school districts take up the largest portion—often 40% to 60%—of a property tax bill. 
- For example Ohio funds its public schools mostly through local property taxes instead of state-wide broad-based funding or higher alternative taxes.
