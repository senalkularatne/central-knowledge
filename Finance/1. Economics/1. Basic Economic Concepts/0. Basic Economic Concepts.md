# Basic Economic Concepts

These are the ideas that the rest of economics relies on, so they come first.

Each note builds on the one before it. Resources are limited, so every use of them means giving up another use. Dividing up work and trading is one way people get more from limited resources. Every trade needs an agreed amount of one thing in return for another, and that amount is a price.
