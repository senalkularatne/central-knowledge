# Financial Markets

A financial market brings together people or organizations buying and selling financial instruments. It includes the trading arrangements, participants, and rules, not just a physical location.

The instruments themselves are explained in [Financial Instruments](../6.%20Financial%20Instruments/0.%20Financial%20Instruments.md). This section explains how they are bought and sold.
