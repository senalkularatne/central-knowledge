# Investment Analysis

Investment analysis uses evidence and methods to evaluate an investment. This section holds methods that can be reused for any investment. A conclusion about a particular company or market at a particular time, which can go out of date, belongs in Investment Research.

Gains, losses, uncertainty and diversification are introduced in [Risk and Return](../5.%20Risk%20and%20Return/0.%20Risk%20and%20Return.md).
