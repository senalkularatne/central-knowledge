Japan Exchange Group (JPX)
- Not much 10x growth observed in recent years.
- Focusing exclusively on the NYSE and Nasdaq is highly effective for catching explosive, hyper-growth "10x" anomalies, but abandoning the Japanese market entirely means missing out on one of the strongest structural bull markets of recent years. Over the 2024–2026 window, the S&P 500 climbed roughly 66%, while Japan’s Nikkei 225 exploded by over 111% in local currency terms.

The Case for Keeping Exposure to Japan (JPX)
While individual 10x stocks are rarer in Japan due to strict listing regulations and a more conservative trading culture, Japan offers powerful macro advantages:
• Massive Index Outperformance: Driven by aggressive corporate governance reforms by the Tokyo Stock Exchange (forcing companies to buy back shares and boost return on equity) and healthy inflation, the Nikkei 225 has dramatically outpaced the S&P 500 over the last two years.
• Strong Value Compounders: Titans like Japan's major trading houses (which Warren Buffett famously targeted) or key AI-enablers like Tokyo Electron have provided incredibly reliable, low-beta upside without the extreme downside volatility found in speculative US tech.
