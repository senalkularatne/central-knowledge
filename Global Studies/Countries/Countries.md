# Countries

Here's a practical ranking of countries most important for understanding world history:

#### Tier 1 - Essential
1. **China** - Longest continuous civilization, influenced all of East Asia, major economic/political force across millennia
2. **United Kingdom** - Industrial Revolution, global empire, shaped modern governance and law
3. **Italy/Rome** - Foundation of Western civilization, law, Christianity, Renaissance
4. **Greece** - Democracy, philosophy, Western thought origins
5. **Egypt** - Ancient civilization, cultural/scientific innovations, crossroads of Africa/Asia/Europe

#### Tier 2 - Critical
6. **France** - Revolution ideals, colonial empire, Enlightenment, European culture
7. **Germany** - Philosophy, both World Wars, Reformation, modern Europe
8. **Spain** - Colonization of Americas, global empire, Islamic-Christian cultural exchange
9. **India** - Ancient civilization, Buddhism/Hinduism, British colonial pivot point
10. **Iraq/Mesopotamia** - Cradle of civilization, writing, agriculture, law codes

#### Tier 3 - Important
11. **Russia/USSR** - Eurasian power, communism, Cold War
12. **USA** - Modern superpower, democracy model, 20th-21st century dominance
13. **Iran/Persia** - Ancient empire, Islam's development, Middle East stability
14. **Japan** - Isolated development, rapid modernization, East Asian power
15. **Turkey/Ottoman Empire** - Bridge between East/West, Islamic caliphate

This ranking prioritizes civilizations with the longest impact, widest influence, and key innovations that shaped human development.
